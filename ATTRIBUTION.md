# Attribution and contribution boundary

The retained **Biohub Harmonic Fusion** tracking system has a historical official score of **0.947**. Its model architectures, pretrained weights and upstream components retain their original credits, including the public pilkwang artifact releases and RoyerLab competition work. The research and integration contributions described in this portfolio are listed below.

The project also used the public synthetic lineage resource released by **José Freitas Alves Neto** during controlled lineage and transfer experiments. That resource and its generator remain credited to their author and are not redistributed here.

Grouped temporal-association and division studies were informed by publicly described Biohub organizer architectures and Trackastra-style temporal association ideas. The portfolio does not claim independent authorship of those systems.

The prediction-diversity audit examined saved notebooks by **Anvith Pothula (anvithpothula)**, **Raunak Dey (raunakdey07)**, and **Kunal Desale (kunaldesale2408)**. Their complete saved prediction outputs were found to be byte-identical in the audited versions. This portfolio does not claim those public predictions as original work.

The higher-order association branch used the public **RoyerLab HOCT** implementation and released pretrained model weights. HOCT remains attributed to its authors; the project contribution was the frozen-detection evaluation design, AWS integration, provenance checks, and source-preserving comparison framework. No successful HOCT metric gain is claimed at the publication cutoff.

Alvaro Mendizabal built and evaluated the development candidate that reached **0.948059** on the reused local cohort. It integrated changes around the retained tracking system while preserving upstream source attribution. The candidate later scored **0.946** officially; that adverse result remains in the record.

Alvaro Mendizabal's contributions demonstrated here include original representation engineering, grouped temporal and parental-association experiments, exact/native metric reconciliation, event-level error attribution, source-preserving graph integration, baseline-locked promotion design, prediction-fidelity auditing, output-lineage comparison, inference/runtime optimization, numerical/workflow safeguards, AWS execution, bounded-cost research orchestration, and technical analysis of measured results.

The external synthetic dataset, pretrained weights, audited public notebooks, HOCT architecture and organizer code retain their respective author and license credits. Integration and evaluation of these components do not transfer their copyright.

Selected figures are genuine saved outputs of completed project analyses. Working datasets, exact feature recipes, tuned thresholds, private AWS state, model weights, caches, checkpoints, candidate graphs, return bundles, and executable submission logic are intentionally not redistributed.
