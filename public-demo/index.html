Reproduce the demo ↗
  1. 01
    Generate a controlled sequence
    Cell coordinates and rendered frames form a public fixture, with explicitly annotated events.
  2. 02
    Choose associations under constraints
    The Python solver selects links from geometry and appearance scores. Compare its policies with an independent-link baseline.
  3. 03
    Inspect topology and actual errors
    A structurally valid graph can still link the wrong cells. Reference-edge and division metrics make that distinction visible.
